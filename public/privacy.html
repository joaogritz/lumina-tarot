Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Lumina Tarot is a personal, open-source project. It has no accounts, uses no tracking cookies and has no analytics or ads. This page explains what data passes through the site, where it goes and how you can control it.

What stays on your device

DataWhere
Reading journal: questions, cards, interpretations and notesBrowser localStorage (up to 100 readings)
Saved tables from the 3D Free TableBrowser IndexedDB
Preferences: theme, deck, language and soundBrowser localStorage
Site files for offline useService worker cache

This data is not sent to any server and is not encrypted. Anyone using the same browser can see it. Clearing the site’s data deletes everything; to keep your journal, use Export in the Journal.

The site’s language is chosen from your browser’s language preferences, read on your device. If you change the language in the menu, your choice is kept in the browser.

What is sent to create an interpretation

Interpretations are generated by artificial intelligence. When you ask for an interpretation (a spread or Yes or No), the site sends:

  • the question you typed, if any;
  • the cards drawn, with their position, orientation and meanings;
  • the name and positions of the spread;
  • the site’s language, so the answer comes back in it.

This data goes to our interpretation server, hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers), which writes the text with Cloudflare Workers AI inside Cloudflare’s own network, using open language models. No other company receives your question. The server does not store the question or the answer, and Cloudflare processes the data under its own policy.

Avoid including full names, ID numbers, addresses or health details in your questions.

The question is optional: if the field is empty, only the cards and the spread are sent. If the service is unavailable, the site tells you and offers to try again; your cards stay on the table. Questions about sensitive subjects, such as self-harm, get a supportive answer put together on your device, without sending anything.

Hosting

The site is served by Vercel. Like any web server, Vercel logs technical access data, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service. Fonts and card images are served by the site itself, with no calls to third parties.

About the readings

Readings are symbolic reflections, not predictions. They do not replace medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. If you are struggling or thinking about hurting yourself, reach out to someone you trust or a crisis line: 988 in the US (call or text), Samaritans on 116 123 in the UK and Ireland, or a line listed for your country at findahelpline.com. In immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Your rights

Because the project keeps no personal data on its own servers, you control the data stored on your device: you can export it, delete it in the Journal or clear the site’s data in your browser. For questions about this policy, open an issue in the project repository.
